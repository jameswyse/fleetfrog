Show a repository's working trees on a machine as one outline: each worktree sits under its clone with its branch, changes and T3 Code thread, and selecting one shows its details and actions in place. Merged worktrees gather in a Finished group with a Clean up action that removes them and their branches together, a Switch branch menu replaces the per-branch buttons, and T3 Code threads and repository details move to their own tabs.
