Discard a checkout's uncommitted changes from its More menu, and choose to discard rather than stash changes when switching branches or removing a worktree. Discarded changes go to Cleanup → Trash, where they can be restored until the Trash is emptied. Discarding needs the cleanup tier. Group actions no longer offer Rescan.
